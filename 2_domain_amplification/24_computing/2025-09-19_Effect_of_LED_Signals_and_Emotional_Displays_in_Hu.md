# Effect of LED Signals and Emotional Displays in Human-Robot Shared Workspaces

## summary
This paper investigates the impact of nonverbal communication (LED signals) on human-robot interaction (HRI), specifically in shared workspaces. We developed a robotic system with an LED strip and facial display to convey movement intent through colour-coded signals and facial expressions.
