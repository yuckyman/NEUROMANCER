## summary
This paper evaluates multiple CNN architectures for audio tagging on the Raspberry Pi, comparing their performance across 1D and 2D models from the Pretrained Audio Neural Networks (PANNs) framework, a ConvNeXt-based model adapted for audio classification, and two PANNs-derived networks. It highlights key findings such as efficiency improvements and the potential of using these architectures on resource-constrained devices.
