The vermian-crest angle: a prediction model for foetal cystic posterior fossa anomalies

BackgroundAccurate categorisation of the upward rotation of the foetal cerebellar vermis continues to pose diagnostic challenges in prenatal medicine. We measured the VCA using 3D-US and compared our data with previously published reference values obtained through both 3D-US and MRI.
