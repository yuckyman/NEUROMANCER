# Emergent Social Dynamics of LLM Agents in the El Farol Bar Problem

## summary
We study the emergent social dynamics of Large Language Model (LLM) agents in a spatially extended El Farol Bar problem. The LLMs autonomously navigate the bar, and their decision-making becomes collective. They also exhibit spontaneous motivation to go to the bar, rather than solving the problem completely like humans.
