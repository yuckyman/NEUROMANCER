# The Paradox of Accurate Generative AI Outputs: Hallucinations, Accuracy, and ...

## summary
As Large Language Models (LLMs) permeate everyday decision-making, their epistemic and societal risks demand urgent scrutiny. Hallucinations are a critical challenge with overreliance on accuracy misdiagnoses the problem, leading to the perception of an accurate AI as always producing the right output. This article develops a taxonomy of hallucination types and shows that the paradox is about outputs (faulty or false), individuals (the creators and users), and society (a global community).
