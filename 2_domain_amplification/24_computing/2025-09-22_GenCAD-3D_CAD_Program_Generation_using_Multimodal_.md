## summary
We introduce GenCAD-3D, a multimodal generative framework that utilizes contrastive learning for aligning latent embeddings between CAD and geometric encoders. The core of this approach involves training a model to generate CAD programs from point clouds and meshes by comparing the generated output against real datasets.
