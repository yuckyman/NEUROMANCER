# Vision-Language Models: A Comprehensive Evaluation and Optimization

## summary
This note presents a comprehensive evaluation of vision-language (VL) models, focusing on their performance under real-world data corruption. It highlights the challenges of model understanding and suggests advancements in VL benchmark datasets, DA methods, and expertise-driven solutions.

Abstract: This paper presents the development of an interactive system for constructing Augmented Virtual Environments (AVEs) by fusing mobile phone images with open-source geospatial data. By integrating 2D image data with 3D models derived from sources such as OpenStreetMap (OSM) and Digital Terrain Models (DTM), the proposed system generates immersive environments that enhance situational context. The system leverages Python for data processing and Unity for 3D visualization, interconnected via UDP-based two-way communication. Preliminary user evaluation demonstrates that the resulting AVEs accurately represent real-world scenes and improve users' contextual understanding.
