# Dual Actor DDPG for Airborne STAR-RIS Assisted Communications

## summary
We propose a novel multi-user downlink communication system in which UAVs and a STAR-RIS (Airborne Simultaneous Transmit and Reflect Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface) are used. The key contributions include joint optimization of UAV trajectory, active beamforming vectors at the base station, and passive RIS TRCs to enhance efficiency while considering UAV energy constraints.
