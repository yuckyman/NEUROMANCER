# Generalizable Geometric Image Caption Synthesis

## summary
This paper introduces a novel method to bridge the gap between multimodal large language models and geometric image understanding by using Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards. By refining captions for geometric images synthesized from 50 basic geometric relations, it can help the model generalize beyond predefined templates.
