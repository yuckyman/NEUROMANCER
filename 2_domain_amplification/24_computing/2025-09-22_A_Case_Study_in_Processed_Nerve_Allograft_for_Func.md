# A Case Study in Processed Nerve Allograft for Functional Recovery of Ulnar Nerve

## summary
We describe a case study involving the use of processed nerve allografts in reconstructive procedures for ulnar nerves. The primary goal was to improve functional recovery following an acute trauma.
