# OpenHA: A Series of Open-Source Hierarchical Agentic Models in Minecraft

## summary
The paper introduces OpenHA, a series of open-source hierarchical autonomous agents (HAs) for Minecraft. The model uses Chain of Action (CoA), which unifies high-level planning and low-level control within a single monolithic VLA model.
