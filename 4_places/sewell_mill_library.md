## quick facts
- opened: dec 2017 (replaced east marietta library)  
- address: 2051 lower roswell rd, marietta, ga 30068  
- phone: (770) 509-4988  
- hours:  
  - mon-wed: 10am – 8pm  
  - thu-fri: 10am – 6pm  
  - sat: 10am – 5pm  
  - sun: closed

## creative studios
- audio recording studio + sound booths  
- video production room w/ green screen  
- hardware/software for creative projects (editing, design, etc.)  
- booking info: [creative studios @ sewell mill](https://www.cobbcounty.gov/library/locations/sewell-mill-library/creative-studios)

## preservation place
digitization service for old photos, tapes, documents, etc. available here + at the georgia room.  
[learn more](https://www.cobbcounty.gov/library/learning-programs/georgia-room/preservationplace)
