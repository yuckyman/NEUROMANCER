Ten quick tips for protecting health data using de-identification and perturbation of structured datasets

by Tshikala Eddie Lulamba, Themba Mutemaringa, Nicki Tiffin Structured patient data generated within the health data ecosystem are shared both internally for operational use and also externally for research and public health benefit. Protecting individual privacy and health data confidentiality in these contexts relies on data de-identification and anonymisation, although there are no universally accepted standards for these processes and the techniques involved can be technically complex. We present practical recommendations grounded in the principle of data minimisation—avoiding unnecessary granularity and identifying variables that could lead to re-identification when combined with other datasets.
