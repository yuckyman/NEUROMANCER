The survey explores two complementary perspectives of how GenAI can be integrated into wireless sensing pipelines:

1. As a plugin to augment task-specific models: This approach involves leveraging GenAI's ability to synthesize high-fidelity data to improve the performance of specific tasks within the pipeline.
2. As a solver to directly address sensing tasks: This method focuses on using GenAI to solve problems that are not directly related to specific tasks but can still benefit from its capabilities.

The article provides an overview of these two perspectives and discusses their potential applications in various wireless sensing scenarios, such as device localization, human activity recognition, and environmental monitoring.'
