The study indicates that V1 neurons might selectively reinforce activity patterns that match prior expectations, potentially aiding in understanding and interpreting visual stimuli.'

Nature Neuroscience, Published online: 15 September 2025; doi:10.1038/s41593-025-02055-5Neurons that respond emergently to illusory contours drive pattern completion in V1. Pattern completion in lower cortical areas may therefore mediate perceptual inference by selectively reinforcing activity patterns that match prior expectations.
