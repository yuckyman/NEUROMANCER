# Recent Articles on Corpus Callosotomies (2020-2024, Open Access)

## Articles

1. **Corpus Callosotomy: Editorial** (Jul 2022) - Akihiro Fujimoto et al.  
   Summary: Reviews history and advances in CC for epilepsy.  
   URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9408211/

2. **Surgical Aspects of Corpus Callosotomy** (Dec 2021) - Takeshi Uda et al.  
   Summary: Details techniques and outcomes for epilepsy treatment.  
   URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8701948/

3. **Corpus Callosotomy for Controlling Epileptic Spasms** (Dec 2021) - Tohru Okanishi et al.  
   Summary: Proposes EEG criteria for patient selection.  
   URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8701945/

4. **Corpus Callosotomy for Refractory Epileptic Spasms: Meta-Analysis** (Dec 2024) - Ryan Whitney et al.  
   Summary: 60-70% seizure reduction efficacy.  
   URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39577169/

5. **Radiosurgical Corpus Callosotomy** (Jul 2023) - Hamdi H et al.  
   Summary: Gamma Knife efficacy in 19 patients.  
   URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36861968/

6. **The Evolution of Corpus Callosotomy** (Jan 2021) - Abhijith Vaddiparti et al.  
   Summary: Historical and modern techniques.  
   URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32889189/

7. **Corpus Callosotomy in the Modern Era** (Mar 2022) - Christopher Markosian et al.  
   Summary: Efficacy, variations, and indications.  
   URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35033693/
