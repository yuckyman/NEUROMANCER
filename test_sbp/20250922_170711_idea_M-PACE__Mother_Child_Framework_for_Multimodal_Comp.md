summary: 'The article discusses the challenges faced by traditional compliance frameworks when it comes to ensuring multi-modal content adheres to brand, legal, or platform-specific standards. These frameworks typically rely on disjointed pipelines that integrate separate modules for image classification, text extraction, audio transcription, hand-crafted checks, and rule-based merges.

To address this challenge, a new framework called Multimodal Parameter Agnostic Compliance Engine (M-PACE) has been proposed. M-PACE aims to unify these workflows under a single, general-purpose framework that can jointly process visual and textual content. The key features of M-PACE include:

1. Unifying the workflow: It allows for a unified approach to processing multi-modal content.
2. Parameter agnosticism: It enables different models or systems to be used independently, without being tied to specific parameters or configurations.
3. Universal handling: It can process various types of inputs (e.g., images, videos, audio) and text.

The main contribution of M-PACE is that it aims to simplify the process of assessing attributes across vision-language inputs in a single pass, thereby reducing operational overhead, improving scalability, and enabling efficient adaptation to dynamic guidelines.'

arXiv:2509.15241v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Ensuring that multi-modal content adheres to brand, legal, or platform-specific compliance standards is an increasingly complex challenge across domains. Traditional compliance frameworks typically rely on disjointed, multi-stage pipelines that integrate separate modules for image classification, text extraction, audio transcription, hand-crafted checks, and rule-based merges. This architectural fragmentation increases operational overhead, hampers scalability, and hinders the ability to adapt to dynamic guidelines efficiently. With the emergence of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), there is growing potential to unify these workflows under a single, general-purpose framework capable of jointly processing visual and textual content. In light of this, we propose Multimodal Parameter Agnostic Compliance Engine (M-PACE), a framework designed for assessing attributes across vision-language inputs in a single pass.
