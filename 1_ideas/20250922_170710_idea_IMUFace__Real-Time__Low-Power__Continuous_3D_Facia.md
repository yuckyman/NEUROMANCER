arXiv:2501.02177v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The potential of facial expression reconstruction technology is significant, with applications in various fields such as human-computer interaction, affective computing, and virtual reality. Recent studies have proposed using ear-worn devices for facial expression reconstruction to address the environmental limitations and privacy concerns associated with traditional camera-based methods. However, these approaches still require improvements in terms of aesthetics and power consumption. This paper introduces a system called IMUFace. It uses inertial measurement units (IMUs) embedded in wireless earphones to detect subtle ear movements caused by facial muscle activities, allowing for covert and low-power facial reconstruction. A user study involving 12 participants was conducted, and a deep learning model named IMUTwinTrans was proposed.
