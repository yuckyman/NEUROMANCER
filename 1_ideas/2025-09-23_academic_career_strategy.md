# Academic & Career Strategy - Comp Sci Master's to PhD

## 🎓 Comp Sci Master's Optimization

### Study Strategies (Aligned with Working Style)
- **Project-based learning**: Don't just read papers, implement them (paper → practice loop)
- **Documentation-first**: Create obsidian notes for each course, link concepts across domains
- **AI-assisted learning**: Use local-deep-research to connect coursework to neuro interests
- **Pivot-friendly**: When hitting walls, switch to different angle (path-of-least-resistance approach)

### Course Selection Priorities
- **ML/AI**: Focus on math foundations, not just frameworks
- **Systems**: Distributed systems, databases (for self-hosting obsession)
- **Theory**: Algorithms, complexity (for systems thinking)
- **Electives**: Anything neuro-adjacent, computational biology

## 🚀 Career Milestones & Pitfalls

### Short-term (1-2 years)
- **Milestone**: Build portfolio of neuro-AI projects (EEG analysis, BCI demos)
- **Pitfall**: Don't get lost in tooling (breadth creep tendency)
- **Strategy**: Pick 2-3 projects max, see them through to completion

### Medium-term (3-5 years)
- **Milestone**: Become the "neuro-AI tooling" person in your field
- **Pitfall**: Don't get pigeonholed as just a tools person
- **Strategy**: Balance research depth with practical impact

### Long-term (5+ years)
- **Milestone**: Lead research at intersection of AI and neuroscience
- **Pitfall**: Don't lose touch with fundamental science
- **Strategy**: Maintain both theoretical depth and practical skills

## 🇪🇺 European PhD Prospects (AI + CS + Neuro)

### Top Programs
- **ETH Zurich**: Strong in both AI and neuro, excellent funding
- **KTH Stockholm**: Robotics + AI, very international
- **TU Delft**: Human-computer interaction, BCI research
- **University of Edinburgh**: Informatics + neuroscience
- **Max Planck Institute**: Various locations, world-class research

### Application Strategy
- **Research fit**: Find labs doing exactly what you want to do
- **Funding**: European PhDs often fully funded (no debt!)
- **Language**: Most programs in English, but learn local language
- **Timeline**: Applications usually due 6-12 months before start

### Competitive Advantages
- Neuro-AI project portfolio
- Self-hosting/technical skills
- Knowledge management systems (shows research organization)
- Vision of brain-CS convergence

## 🧠 ADHD-Adjacent Dopamine Optimization

### Study/Work Strategies
- **Pomodoro with AI breaks**: 25 min focus, 5 min chat with AI about what you learned
- **Project switching**: When hitting walls, switch to different project (pivot approach)
- **Immediate feedback**: Use AI to get instant answers to questions
- **Visual progress**: Track learning with obsidian graphs and progress bars

### Environmental Hacks
- **Headphones + coffee**: Already known to work
- **Change locations**: Basement → coffee shop → library (novelty = dopamine)
- **Social accountability**: Find study buddy or join research group
- **Reward systems**: Complete module → play Pokémon for 30 min

### Medication Considerations
- **Stimulants**: Can help with focus but watch for hyperfocus on wrong things
- **Non-stimulants**: Strattera, Wellbutrin (less crash, more consistent)
- **Lifestyle**: Exercise, sleep, nutrition (boring but effective)
