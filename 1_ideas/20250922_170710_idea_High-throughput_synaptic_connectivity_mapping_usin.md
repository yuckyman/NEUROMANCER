summary: 'The article describes a study that used two-photon optical gene editing (two-photon optogenetics) to map the connections between neurons in the mouse visual cortex. The researchers recorded electrical activity from individual neurons and reconstructed the neural signals using sparse signal reconstruction techniques. They then used these methods to identify specific connections between neurons, providing insights into how different parts of the brain are connected.'
