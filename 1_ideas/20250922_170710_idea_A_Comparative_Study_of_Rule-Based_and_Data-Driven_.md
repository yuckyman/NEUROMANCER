summary: 'The article discusses the differences between traditional rule-based architectures and machine learning and artificial intelligence (ML & AI) based industrial monitoring systems. It presents a comparison of these two methodologies, highlighting their strengths, limitations, and application scenarios.

Rule-based systems are known for their high interpretability, deterministic behavior, and ease of implementation in stable environments. However, they face challenges with scalability, adaptability, and performance in complex or evolving contexts. Data-driven systems excel in detecting hidden anomalies and enabling predictive maintenance and dynamic adaptation to new conditions. Despite their high accuracy, these models have limitations such as difficulty in interpreting the data, potential for bias, and the need for continuous learning.

The article suggests a basic framework to evaluate the key properties of both types of systems, providing insights into their strengths and weaknesses. It also discusses how these differences can be used to guide the design and implementation of industrial monitoring systems in Industry 4.0 environments.'
