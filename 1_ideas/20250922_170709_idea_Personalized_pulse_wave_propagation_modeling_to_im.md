title: Personalized pulse wave propagation modeling to improve vasopressor dosing
  management in patients with severe traumatic brain injury
link: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013501
summary: 'The study investigates whether examining the shape of arterial pulse waves and fitting them to a physiology-based mathematical model can provide additional insights into the state of the cardiovascular system in patients with severe traumatic brain injury (sTBI). The authors conducted a longitudinal study on 25 sTBI patients in an intensive care unit. Non-invasively, they recorded non-invasive arterial pulse waves from wrists and ankles using an oscillometric method. These waveforms were then used to inform a 0-1D model of the arterial blood flow dynamics. The authors then modeled patient-specific cardiovascular parameters based on these waveforms and predicted changes in the administered dose of vasopressor (norepinephrine) in the next 24 hours using their mathematical model. The study found that the shape of the pulse waves could provide valuable insights into the state of the cardiovascular system, but the model's accuracy was not as high as expected based on previous studies.'
